Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

📅 Last updated: 1 March 2025

At Ember Butter Plates (accessible at emberbutterplates.com), the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at hello@emberbutterplates.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you voluntarily provide, and information automatically collected through your use of the site.

Information you provide to us

  • Contact forms: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Email correspondence: If you email us directly, we may retain the content of your message and our reply.
  • Comments (if enabled): If you leave a comment on a recipe, we collect the data shown in the comment form (name, email, comment) and your IP address to help spam detection.

Information automatically collected

  • Log data: Like most websites, we collect standard server log information, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of visits, and referring pages.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and to record user-specific information on which pages the user accesses. See our Cookies Policy for details.
  • Analytics: We may use third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with the site. These services may collect data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

🍪 A note about cookies: You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings. This won't break your ability to read our recipes, but some interactive features may not work as intended.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To respond to your emails, questions, and requests.
  • To improve our website and recipe content based on visitor feedback and usage patterns.
  • To send you occasional updates, if you have explicitly opted in to our newsletter (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To prevent fraudulent activity and ensure the security of our site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Log Files

Ember Butter Plates follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information. It is used for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Ember Butter Plates uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

For more detailed information, please read our Cookies Policy.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Ember Butter Plates' Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We may include links to third-party sites (for example, ingredient suppliers or affiliate partners). We have no control over those sites and their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit through a link on our site.

Some third-party services we may use include:

  • Google Analytics — for understanding site traffic. You can review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Email service providers — if you subscribe to our newsletter, your email is stored with a reputable provider (e.g., Mailchimp, ConvertKit) that complies with data protection laws.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Contact form submissions are typically kept for 12 months, after which they are deleted. If you wish to have your data removed sooner, please contact us.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access — You can request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification — You can request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure — You can request that we delete your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing — You can request that we restrict the processing of your data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to object to processing — You can object to our processing of your data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to data portability — You can request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@emberbutterplates.com.

8. Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best to promptly remove such information from our records.

9. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will post any changes on this page and, if the changes are significant, we will provide a more prominent notice (such as an email notification if you have subscribed). We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Questions? If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our contact page or email hello@emberbutterplates.com.
